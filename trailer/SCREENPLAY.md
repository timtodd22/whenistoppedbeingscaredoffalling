# THE LONG RAIN — teaser trailer (61 s)

An unofficial fan film set in the Blade Runner world, 2099.
Not affiliated with or endorsed by Alcon Entertainment, Amazon MGM Studios, Warner Bros., or anyone involved in the Blade Runner films or series.
All characters, dialogue and music are original.

Written in the spirit of Hampton Fancher's laconic, poetic noir; shot list built on Denis Villeneuve's visual grammar (monumental scale, silence, haze, silhouettes, slow deliberate camera).
Neither is involved, and the trailer carries no credits in their names.

## Logline

A replicant built to last four years has lived seventy. The young blade runner sent to retire her has one real question: how.

## Characters

- **IRIS VANCE**, replicant, early seventies in appearance. Silver-white cropped hair, deep lines, unhurried, amused by fear.
- **TOBIN HALE**, blade runner, late twenties. Rain-dark wool coat, very still, eyes that have stopped expecting answers.

## Look

- 2.39:1 inside 16:9 (1920×1080 with bars), 24 fps.
- Palette per world: sea wall (cold steel blue); orange dust (ochre, nearly monochrome); city (cyan and magenta neon over sodium orange); interiors (hard tungsten, deep black).
- Lenses: wide shots 24–35 mm, close-ups 85–100 mm; slow push-ins, no handheld.
- Haze everywhere: volumetric fog in Blender, matched with grain and halation on the AI shots.

## Sound

- Object-based mix: every element has a 3D position over time.
- Main deliverable: binaural (headphones). Also a 7.1.4 12-channel WAV rendered from the same objects.
- Original score: analogue-style synth brass and pads, low brass hits, and a single sustained tone.
